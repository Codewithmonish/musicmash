# Check your tuning reference first

Before working on your ear, rule out the simplest cause: the instrument (or the backing
track) is not tuned to A4 = 440 Hz.

## Checklist
1. Tune with a tuner set to 440 Hz. Some tuner apps default to 442 Hz or have been changed.
2. For string instruments, re-check after five minutes of playing: new strings and temperature
   changes make tuning slip.
3. If you play along with a recording, check its tuning: many older recordings are a few
   cents away from 440 Hz.
4. If every note in your analysis is off by roughly the same amount in the same direction,
   it is very likely a reference problem rather than a technique problem.

## What to do next
Re-tune, record the same passage again, and compare the mean signed deviation. If it moves
close to zero, the issue was the reference. If it stays, move on to the drone and slow-scale
exercises.

**How long:** 2 minutes before every practice session.
