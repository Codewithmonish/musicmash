# Subdivision counting

Rushing and uneven notes often come from thinking only in beats. Feeling the smaller units
inside each beat (subdivisions) keeps the space between notes even.

## Exercise
1. Metronome on quarter notes at a slow tempo.
2. Count out loud "1-e-and-a, 2-e-and-a" (sixteenths) while clapping only on the beats.
3. Now clap on every sixteenth while still counting.
4. Play your passage while counting the subdivisions in your head. Long notes are where
   people rush most: count through them.
5. Set the metronome to click on eighth notes, then sixteenths, for the hardest bars.

## Tip
If a rest or a long note is followed by fast notes, it is very common to cut the long note
short. Counting the subdivisions makes the hold the right length.

**How long:** 10 minutes a day.
