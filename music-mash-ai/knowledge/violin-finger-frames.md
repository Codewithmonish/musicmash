# Violin: finger frames

Good violin intonation comes from a stable hand frame, so that fingers fall in the same places
every time.

## Exercise
1. Place 1st finger and 4th finger on the A string so 4th matches the open E string. This
   octave/fourth frame is your reference.
2. Lower the 2nd and 3rd fingers inside the frame for the scale pattern you need (whole and half
   steps).
3. Lift and replace the whole frame several times without moving the hand.
4. Play the scale slowly with a drone, checking 4th finger against the next open string.

## Tips
- Half steps should be close together, with fingers touching.
- Check notes that ring with open strings (sympathetic resonance): they "glow" when in tune.

**How long:** 10 minutes a day.
