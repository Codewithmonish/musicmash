# Gap click: build an internal pulse

Once you can play with a metronome, the next step is keeping time without it. A "gap click"
plays for a few bars and then goes silent for a few bars.

## Exercise
1. Set a metronome (many apps have a "mute bars" or "gap" setting) to 2 bars on, 2 bars off.
2. Play your passage. When the click returns, notice whether you are early (rushing) or late
   (dragging).
3. When you land exactly with the returning click three times in a row, use 2 on, 4 off.
4. Alternative: set the click on beats 2 and 4 only, so you must feel 1 and 3 yourself.

## Why it helps
Steady timing in a performance comes from the pulse you carry inside, not from the click.

**How long:** 10 minutes, after the basic timing exercises feel easy.
