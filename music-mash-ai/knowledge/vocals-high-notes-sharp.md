# Vocals: sharp on high notes

Singers often go sharp on high or loud notes because they push with too much breath pressure or
tension in the throat.

## Exercise
1. Sing the high phrase on a lip trill or "ng" hum first. These reduce pressure.
2. Sing it on "oo" at a medium volume, then at your performance volume.
3. Think "down" as you go up: keep the larynx and jaw relaxed and the space in the mouth tall.
4. Check the highest note with a tuner; if it is sharp, sing it more softly until it is centred,
   then build the volume back up.

## Tip
Loud should come from support, not squeezing. If your neck muscles tighten, the pitch will rise.

**How long:** 10 minutes, only after warming up.
