# Why slow practice works

Slow practice is not just "playing it slower". It is practising with enough time to notice and
control every note.

## Principles
- **Slow enough to be perfect**: if you make the same mistake twice, the tempo is too fast.
- **Same motions as fast**: keep the fingering, bowing and breathing you will use at full speed.
- **Listen to every note**: intonation and timing problems you cannot hear at speed become
  obvious at half speed.
- **Short sessions**: 10 focused minutes beat 40 distracted ones.

## Exercise
1. Play the problem passage at 50% of the target tempo.
2. After each run, name one thing that was not perfect, and fix only that on the next run.
3. Increase speed only after three perfect runs in a row.

**How long:** Use this method for any passage flagged in your analysis.
