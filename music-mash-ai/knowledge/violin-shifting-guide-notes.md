# Violin: shifting with guide notes

Many intonation errors on violin happen on shifts, where the hand moves to a new position.

## Exercise
1. Play the note before the shift, then slide slowly on the same finger to the new position
   (the "guide note"), and only then place the target finger.
2. Make the slide audible at first; later, make it faster and lighter until it disappears.
3. Repeat each shift five times, checking the arrival note with a tuner or drone.
4. Play the passage at tempo with an inaudible shift.

## Tip
Release the thumb and finger pressure during the shift: the hand should glide, not drag.

**How long:** 10 minutes on the shifts in your piece.
