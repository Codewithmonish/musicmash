# Hands separately, then together

When hands play together, the harder hand often pulls the timing. Separating them makes it clear
which hand is the problem.

## Exercise
1. Play the passage left hand alone with the metronome. Then the right hand alone.
2. Note which hand rushes or hesitates.
3. Play hands together very slowly, focusing on the weaker hand.
4. Play the left hand while tapping the right-hand rhythm on your leg, then swap.

## Tip
The left hand often provides the pulse. If it is steady, the right hand can be more expressive
without the overall tempo drifting.

**How long:** 10–15 minutes on the passage.
