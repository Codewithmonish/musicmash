# Missing notes: chunk the passage

When a comparison with the original shows "missing notes", you are playing fewer notes than the
reference in that section. Usually a fast run is being smudged or skipped.

## Exercise: chunking
1. Split the section into chunks of 3–5 notes.
2. Play each chunk slowly until it is clean three times in a row.
3. Join chunk 1 and 2, then 2 and 3, and so on, always overlapping by one note.
4. Play the full section slowly, then use the metronome ladder to bring it up to tempo.

## Check
Listen to the reference at half speed (most media players can do this) and count the notes in
the section. Make sure you know every note before speeding up.

**How long:** 10–15 minutes on the section, daily.
