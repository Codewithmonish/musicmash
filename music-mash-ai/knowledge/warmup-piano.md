# Piano warm-up

## Routine
1. **Five-finger patterns** (C-D-E-F-G) in each hand, slowly, with a relaxed wrist and curved
   fingers. Listen for every note being equally loud.
2. **Scales**, hands separately, then together, in the key of your piece, with a metronome.
3. **Arpeggios** of the main chords of your piece.
4. **Rhythmic evenness**: play the scale in dotted rhythms, then evenly again; the evenness
   usually improves immediately.

## Tips
- Keep the shoulders down and the forearm loose; tension makes notes uneven.
- Use the same fingering every time.

**How long:** 10 minutes.
