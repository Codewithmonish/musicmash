# Hear it before you play it

Many intonation errors happen because the player has not decided what the next note should
sound like. "Audiation" means hearing a pitch in your head before producing it.

## Exercise
1. Take a four-bar phrase from your piece.
2. Sing it (quietly is fine, any syllable) before playing it. If singing is hard, hum.
3. Play the first note, then stop. Imagine the second note clearly, then play it. Continue
   note by note through the phrase.
4. Play the phrase normally. Then play it once more with a drone underneath.

## Why it helps
Your hands and voice aim at whatever target your ear provides. A clear target means smaller
corrections, so the measured deviation drops and notes settle faster.

**How long:** 5–10 minutes per phrase, focusing on the phrases where your problem sections
were detected.
