# Planning a practice week

A good practice week mixes focused work on your weakest area with maintenance of everything else.

## Structure
- **Every day**: 10-minute warm-up, then 15–20 minutes on your top issue, then 5–10 minutes
  playing music for enjoyment.
- **Rotate issues**: if you have three issues, spend two days each on the first two and one day
  on the third, then a review day.
- **Record twice a week**: day 1 and day 7 at least, so you can compare measured progress.
- **One rest day** or a light day: your brain consolidates skills while you rest.

## Tips
- Write down the tempo you reached each day.
- Change one thing at a time so you know what helped.

**How long:** 30–40 minutes a day is enough for steady improvement.
