# Even scales on piano

On piano, pitch is fixed, so the main measurable issues are timing and evenness. Uneven scales
usually come from the thumb passing under too late.

## Exercise
1. Metronome at a slow tempo, scale in sixteenths, hands separately.
2. Start moving the thumb under as soon as it has played, not at the last moment.
3. Accent every fourth note, then every third note (this shifts where the accent falls and
   forces evenness).
4. Play without accents and listen for a perfectly even stream.

## Tip
Record the scale and listen: a small hitch at the thumb crossing is the most common problem.

**How long:** 10 minutes a day.
