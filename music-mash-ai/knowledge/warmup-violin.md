# Violin warm-up

## Routine
1. **Open strings**: whole bows, 4 beats each at 60 BPM, steady contact point.
2. **One-octave scale** in first position with a drone on the tonic, slowly.
3. **Finger patterns**: 0-1-2-3-4 on each string, checking 4th finger against the next open
   string (they should match exactly).
4. **Shifting**: slide slowly between first and third position on one finger, landing in tune.

## Tips
- Use open strings as built-in tuners: a stopped note that matches an open string rings more.
- Keep the left thumb relaxed; a squeezing thumb tightens the whole hand.

**How long:** 10 minutes.
