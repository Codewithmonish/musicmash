# Record, listen, compare to the grid

Players almost always judge their own timing as better than it is while playing. Recording and
reviewing is the most reliable way to improve.

## Exercise
1. Record the passage with the metronome audible in the recording.
2. Listen back once just for timing (ignore wrong notes). Mark each bar where a note was clearly
   early or late.
3. Practise only the marked bars, slowly, with the click.
4. Record again and compare your timing error and your percentage of notes within 30 ms.

## Tip
Keep the recordings. Comparing a recording from day 1 with one from day 7 is the clearest
evidence of progress, and it is very motivating.

**How long:** 15 minutes, every two or three days.
