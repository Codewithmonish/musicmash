# Long notes with a steady pitch

Pitch that wanders within a held note (a high "pitch stability" value in cents) sounds
uncertain even when the average pitch is right. The fix is controlled long-tone practice.

## Exercise: long tones
1. Choose a comfortable note in the middle of your range.
2. Hold it for 8 beats at 60 BPM against a drone or tuner.
3. Keep the pitch completely still: no swell, no fade, no vibrato for now.
4. Move up by step and repeat across one octave.
5. Then do the same at a soft level, which is harder and more revealing.

## What to listen for
- Pitch rising when you get louder or sinking when you get softer.
- A dip at the very start (the attack) or at the very end (the release).

**How long:** 5–10 minutes at the start of each session. It doubles as a warm-up.
