# Anchor points against tempo drift

Tempo drift means the second half of a performance is faster or slower than the first. It
happens gradually, so you rarely notice it while playing.

## Exercise
1. Choose 3–4 "anchor points" in the piece: the start of each section.
2. Before you start, set the tempo in your head by listening to the metronome for 8 beats.
3. Play through. At each anchor point, briefly turn the metronome on (or have a practice
   partner tap) and see whether you still match.
4. Note which section drifts most and give that section extra practice with the click.

## Tip
Most players speed up in loud or exciting sections and slow down in quiet or difficult ones.
Knowing your own pattern is half the fix.

**How long:** One full run-through a day with anchor checks.
