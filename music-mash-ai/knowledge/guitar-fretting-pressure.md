# Guitar: pressing too hard makes notes sharp

On guitar, pressing a string too hard stretches it and raises the pitch. This is a very common
reason for a consistently sharp reading.

## Exercise
1. Fret a note and play it. Slowly release pressure until the note buzzes.
2. Add just enough pressure to stop the buzz. That is all the pressure you need.
3. Play a scale with this light pressure, checking a few notes with a tuner.
4. Press the fingertip just behind the fret (not in the middle of the fret space).

## Also check
- Don't push or pull the string sideways while fretting (that bends the pitch).
- Very high action or heavy strings make this worse; a setup by a technician can help.

**How long:** 5 minutes a day until light pressure is automatic.
