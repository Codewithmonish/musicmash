# Vocals: sirens and pitch matching

Singing in tune starts with matching a pitch you hear and feeling the small adjustments that
bring you to the centre.

## Exercise
1. Siren slowly on "oo" from low to high and back, smoothly, like a fire engine.
2. Play a note on a keyboard. Start a siren below it and slide up until you match it exactly,
   then hold for 4 beats.
3. Do the same starting above the note and sliding down.
4. Sing five-note scales, checking the 3rd and 5th notes with a tuner.

## Tips
- Matching from below and above teaches you which way to correct.
- Sing quietly; it is easier to hear the reference.

**How long:** 10 minutes a day.
