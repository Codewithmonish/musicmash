# The metronome ladder

A metronome ladder builds speed without letting the pulse slip. It is the standard remedy for
passages that rush or drag.

## Exercise
1. Find the tempo at which you can play the passage perfectly with the click. Write it down.
2. Play it three times in a row cleanly at that tempo.
3. Raise the metronome by 4 BPM and repeat. If you make a mistake, drop back 8 BPM.
4. Stop when you reach the target tempo or after 15 minutes, whichever comes first.
5. The next day, start 8 BPM below yesterday's top tempo.

## Rules
- Clean means in time with the click, not just the right notes.
- If you notice yourself landing before the click, you are rushing: slow down one step.

**How long:** 10–15 minutes on one passage per day.
