# Vocal warm-up

Singers need to warm up the body, the breath and the ear before singing repertoire.

## Routine
1. **Body**: roll the shoulders, stretch the neck gently, massage the jaw.
2. **Breath**: four slow breaths in low, out on a steady "sss" for 10–20 seconds.
3. **Lip trills or "ng" hums** on slides up and down a fifth. These relax the voice and connect
   the registers.
4. **Five-note scales** on "mee-may-mah-moh-moo", moving up by half steps, then down.
5. **Pitch matching**: play a note on a keyboard, sing it, and hold it while checking with a tuner.

## Tips
- Stay in a comfortable range at first; extend gradually.
- If anything hurts or feels scratchy, stop and rest.

**How long:** 10–15 minutes.
