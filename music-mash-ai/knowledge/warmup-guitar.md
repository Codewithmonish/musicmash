# Guitar warm-up

## Routine
1. **Chromatic "spider" exercise**: frets 1-2-3-4 on each string, one finger per fret, with a
   metronome at a slow tempo. Alternate picking (down-up) strictly.
2. **Slow scale** in the key of your piece, listening for clear notes without buzz.
3. **Chord changes**: switch between two chords from your piece on every beat, then every half
   beat.
4. **Strumming** with a metronome: steady down-up motion even when you miss strings on purpose.

## Tips
- Check tuning before and after the warm-up.
- Press only as hard as needed for a clean note; extra pressure makes notes sharp.

**How long:** 10 minutes.
