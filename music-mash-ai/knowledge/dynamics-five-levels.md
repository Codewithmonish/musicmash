# Five levels of loudness

Many players have only two dynamic levels: "normal" and "loud". Music needs a wider range.

## Exercise
1. Choose one note. Play it at five levels: pp, p, mf, f, ff. Each level must be clearly
   different from the one before.
2. Do the same with a scale: one level per octave or per four notes.
3. Keep the pitch steady at every level. Getting louder often makes pitch go sharp, and
   getting softer often makes it go flat.
4. Apply the five levels to one phrase of your piece: decide where the loudest note is and
   shape the phrase towards it.

## Measure it
Record the exercise. The level difference between your softest and loudest note should be
large; if it is small, exaggerate more than feels natural.

**How long:** 5–10 minutes a day.
