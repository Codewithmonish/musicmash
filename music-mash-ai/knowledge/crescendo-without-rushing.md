# Crescendo without rushing

Getting louder and getting faster are linked in our instincts, so crescendos often rush (and
diminuendos often drag).

## Exercise
1. Metronome at a slow tempo. Play a scale with a crescendo from p to f over two bars.
2. Keep every note exactly with the click. If you land early as you get louder, slow down.
3. Reverse it: diminuendo from f to p while staying exactly with the click.
4. Apply this to the crescendos in your piece.

## Tip
Think of a crescendo as "more weight" (air, bow, arm), not "more speed".

**How long:** 5 minutes, alongside your timing practice.
