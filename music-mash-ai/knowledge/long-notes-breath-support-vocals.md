# Breath support for steady sung notes

For singers, unsteady or sagging long notes are almost always a breath-management issue.

## Exercise: hiss, then sing
1. Breathe in low (belly and sides expand, shoulders stay down).
2. Hiss on "sss" for as long as you can with a perfectly even stream. Aim for 20 seconds.
3. Now sing a comfortable note on "oo" for 8 slow beats with the same even stream.
4. Repeat on "ah". Watch the tuner: the needle should stay still to the last beat.

## Common mistakes
- Pushing more air at the end to "save" the note: the pitch goes sharp.
- Letting the air collapse at the end: the pitch goes flat.
- Lifting the shoulders on the breath: it adds tension and makes high notes harder.

**How long:** 5 minutes daily, before any repertoire.
