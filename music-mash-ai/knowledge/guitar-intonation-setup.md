# Guitar: is it you or the instrument?

If notes higher up the neck are consistently out of tune while open strings are correct, the
guitar's own intonation (the saddle position) may be off.

## Check
1. Tune the open string precisely.
2. Play the harmonic at the 12th fret, then the fretted note at the 12th fret.
3. If the fretted note is sharper than the harmonic, the saddle needs to move back; if flatter,
   forward. This adjustment is usually done by a technician.
4. Repeat for each string.

## Why it matters
If the instrument is out, no amount of practice will make high notes read in tune. Fix the
instrument first, then work on technique.

**How long:** One check; then re-record and compare.
