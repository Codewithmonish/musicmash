# Vibrato or wobble?

Vibrato is a regular, even oscillation around the centre of the note. A wobble is irregular
and usually drifts away from the centre. Pitch-stability measurements include both, so a
high value is not automatically a problem if your vibrato is intentional and centred.

## How to check
1. Play a long note without vibrato. If it is steady, your base pitch control is fine.
2. Add vibrato. Listen (or watch the pitch curve) for an even rate, roughly 5–7 cycles per
   second, and an equal swing above and below the note.
3. If the swing goes mostly below the note (common on violin), the note will sound flat.

## Exercise
- Metronome at 60 BPM. Do 2, then 3, then 4 vibrato cycles per beat, slowly and evenly.
- Always start the note straight and add vibrato after the pitch is centred.

**How long:** 5 minutes, only after the long-tone exercises are comfortable.
