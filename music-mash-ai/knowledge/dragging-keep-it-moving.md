# Stop dragging

Dragging is the opposite of rushing: the tempo gets slower, usually in hard passages, during
long phrases, or when concentration drops near the end.

## Exercise
1. Identify where the tempo drops (the analysis marks problem sections as "dragging").
2. Play from two bars before that point with the metronome, and keep going for two bars after.
3. Think "forward": feel the next downbeat coming instead of waiting for it.
4. Lighten the sound slightly in the difficult bars. Heavy playing usually slows down.
5. Practise the ending of your piece first, so it is as familiar as the opening.

## Tip
If you drag only in technically hard spots, the passage is not yet secure at tempo. Combine
this with the metronome ladder.

**How long:** 10 minutes a day.
