# Guitar: the strumming pendulum

Steady strumming comes from a hand that never stops moving, like a pendulum.

## Exercise
1. Metronome at 70 BPM. Move the strumming hand down on every beat and up on every "and",
   continuously.
2. Strum all the down-strokes and none of the up-strokes, but keep the up motion going in the
   air.
3. Now hit some up-strokes and miss others (a pattern like D - D U - U D U), still without
   stopping the motion.
4. Apply the pattern to your song's chords.

## Why it helps
When the hand stops between strums, it has to restart, and that is when timing slips (usually
rushing).

**How long:** 10 minutes a day.
