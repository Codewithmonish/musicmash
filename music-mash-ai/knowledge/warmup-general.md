# A 10-minute warm-up for any instrument

A short, consistent warm-up prepares your body and ears and makes the rest of the session more
productive.

## Routine
1. **2 minutes: long tones** on comfortable notes, listening for a steady pitch.
2. **3 minutes: a scale** in the key of your piece, slowly, with a drone or tuner.
3. **3 minutes: rhythm**: the same scale in eighths, triplets and sixteenths with a metronome.
4. **2 minutes: a phrase** from your piece, slowly and musically.

## Tips
- Keep it the same every day so it becomes automatic.
- If something feels tense, stop and shake out your hands, shoulders and jaw.

**How long:** 10 minutes at the start of every session.
