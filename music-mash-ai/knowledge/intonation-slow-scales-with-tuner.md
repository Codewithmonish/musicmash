# Slow scales with a tuner

The fastest way to find out which notes you habitually misplace is to look at them one by
one, slowly, with a tuner.

## Exercise
1. Choose one scale that appears in your piece. Set a metronome to 60 BPM.
2. Play each note for two beats. Look at the tuner only after the note has started, so you
   learn where you naturally land, not where you steer to.
3. Write down any note that lands more than 10 cents off, and in which direction.
4. Play only those notes again: approach each one from the note below and the note above.
5. Finish with the whole scale, eyes closed, then check one last time with the tuner.

## Tips
- Don't stare at the needle while you play. Look, correct, then play the note again without
  looking; the goal is to move the fix into your ears and hands.
- The same note can drift differently going up and coming down. Practise both directions.

**How long:** 10–15 minutes, one scale per day.
