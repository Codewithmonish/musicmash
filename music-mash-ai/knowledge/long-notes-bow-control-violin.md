# Bow control for steady long notes (violin)

On violin, an unsteady bow (changing speed or pressure) changes the sound and can make the
left hand wobble with it.

## Exercise: whole bows on open strings
1. Metronome at 60 BPM. One whole bow (frog to tip) lasts 4 beats.
2. Divide the bow into four equal parts and arrive at each quarter on the beat.
3. Keep the contact point the same distance from the bridge all the way.
4. Repeat with 6 and then 8 beats per bow.
5. Add a stopped note (first finger on the A string) and keep the left hand relaxed while the
   bow does the work.

## Check
Record yourself and look at the pitch curve: a flat line on held notes means bow and left hand
are both steady.

**How long:** 5–10 minutes a day.
